**Data Collection:** `When using our scripts protected with the LuaProt system, certain technical information is automatically collected solely for security, authentication, and access control purposes. This information includes:`

> **IP Address :** collected and processed exclusively by [Luaprot.net](https://luaprot.net/) as part of the access control process. We at Project Aurora, as providers, do not have direct access to this data.<br /><br />**Timestamps :** records of the date and time when the script was executed.<br /><br />**Executor fingerprint (HWID) :** non-sensitive technical identifiers used only for authentication. The HWID refers to the identifier generated and provided by the executor (not a direct hardware ID from your device), while the User-Agent specifies which executor is being used.<br /><br />**Discord ID :** used as part of the authentication process and to link the license to your Discord account.

<Callout icon="x" color="#FF2525">
  Data such as Roblox usernames, account IDs, in-game stats, or sensitive device information is not collected.
</Callout>
