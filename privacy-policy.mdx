This Privacy Policy explains how Project Aurora handles information in connection with our products and services.

<Warning>
  By accessing or using our Services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
</Warning>

## 1. Information & Data Processing

Aurora products use [Luarmor](https://luarmor.net/) for authentication, licensing, and access control.

When using an Aurora product, certain technical information may be collected and processed by [Luarmor](https://luarmor.net/) as part of these services.

This may include:

- **IP Address:** collected and processed by Luarmor as part of its authentication and access-control infrastructure. Project Aurora does not have direct access to IP addresses collected by Luarmor.
- **Timestamps:** information regarding when authentication requests or script executions occur.
- **Executor Fingerprint (HWID):** a technical identifier generated and provided by the executor for authentication and access control. This is not a direct hardware identifier obtained from your physical device.
- **Executor User-Agent:** information used to identify the executor being used and assist with authentication, security, and compatibility.
- **Discord ID:** used to associate your Aurora license with your Discord account and manage access to our Services.

For information regarding data collected and processed directly by [Luarmor](https://luarmor.net/), please refer to the [Luarmor Privacy Policy](https://luarmor.net/tos).

## 2. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Project Aurora does not intentionally collect or require the following information for the operation of our Services:

- Roblox usernames or display names.
- Roblox account IDs.

- In-game statistics or gameplay data.
- Passwords or third-party account credentials.
- Direct hardware identifiers obtained from your physical device.
- Personal files or unrelated device information.

We only use information reasonably necessary for authentication, licensing, security, access control, and operation of our Services.

## 3. Data Sharing

Project Aurora does not sell user information or intentionally share information with third parties for advertising or unrelated commercial purposes.

Information may be processed by third-party providers when necessary to provide authentication, security, script protection, infrastructure, or other functionality required by our Services.

Third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Project Aurora may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our Services, infrastructure, or data practices.

Significant changes may be announced through our official Discord server, website, or other official communication channels.

Your continued use of the Services after an updated Privacy Policy becomes effective constitutes your acknowledgment of the revised policy.
